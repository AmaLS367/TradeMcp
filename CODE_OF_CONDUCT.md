# 📜 Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and professional environment for everyone contributing to and communicating within the Trade MCP ecosystem.

[Our Pledge](#-our-pledge) · [Expected Behavior](#-expected-behavior) · [Unacceptable Behavior](#-unacceptable-behavior) · [Reporting and Enforcement](#-reporting-and-enforcement)

---

## 🤝 Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## 💎 Behavior Guidelines

We strive to maintain a professional, collaborative workspace. Here is what we expect from all participants:

| Expected Behavior | Unacceptable Behavior |
|---|---|
| 💬 **Welcoming Language:** Use inclusive, encouraging, and respectful language in all discussions. | 🚫 **Trolling & Harassment:** Do not post insulting, derogatory, or personally attacking comments. |
| 🙏 **Respect Diversity:** Respect differing viewpoints, technical opinions, and cultural backgrounds. | 🚫 **Personal Attacks:** Avoid any personal or political attacks against community members. |
| 📝 **Constructive Feedback:** Accept constructive criticism gracefully and focus on continuous improvement. | 🚫 **Private Leakage:** Do not publish or share others' private information (such as API keys or personal details). |
| 🌱 **Community First:** Prioritize what is best for the overall health and safety of the open-source community. | 🚫 **Spam & Disruption:** Do not engage in disruptive behavior, advertising, or spam in repository issues. |

---

## 🛡️ Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it immediately by opening a private GitHub issue or contacting the project leads. All complaints will be reviewed, investigated, and will result in a response that is necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.
