# 📜 Code of Conduct

![Contributor Covenant](https://img.shields.io/badge/Contributor%20Covenant-2.1-purple)

---

## 🤝 Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a **harassment-free experience** for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## ✅ Expected Behavior

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| 💬 Use welcoming and inclusive language | 🚫 Trolling or insulting comments |
| 🙏 Respect differing viewpoints | 🚫 Personal or political attacks |
| 📝 Accept constructive criticism | 🚫 Public or private harassment |
| 🌱 Focus on what's best for the community | 🚫 Publishing others' private info |

---

## 🛡️ Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. Instances of abusive behavior may be reported by opening an issue. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
